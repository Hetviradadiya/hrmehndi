Aftercare Guide

Make your mehndi last

Follow these steps for the deepest, most vibrant stain. Colour develops fully within 24–48 hours of paste removal.

01
🍋
Lemon-sugar sealant
Once the mehndi paste dries (1–2 hrs), dab a mix of lemon juice and sugar using a cotton ball. This seals the paste and deepens the colour.
02
🌙
Keep the paste on overnight
The longer the paste stays on, the richer the stain. Aim for 6–8 hours minimum — wrapping with tissue or medical tape helps.
03
✋
Scrape, don't wash
Remove dried paste by gently scraping with a blunt edge. Avoid water for at least 24 hours after removal.
04
🚿
Avoid water & soap
Water and soap lighten the colour dramatically in the first 24 hours. Pat dry if contact is unavoidable; use oil as a barrier.
05
🫙
Apply mustard oil or balm
Rub in mustard oil, coconut oil, or Vicks after scraping. This drives colour into the skin and extends stain life.
06
⏰
Full colour in 24–48 hrs
Your mehndi reaches its deepest, richest red-brown tone between 24–48 hours after paste removal. Colour lasts 10–21 days.
